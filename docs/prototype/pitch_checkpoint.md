## Pitch claim we can support

NextBest is a university prototype that combines short OSHC learning activities,
access to a verified policy source and explanations of synthetic bills. We have
demonstrated the complete workflow and reproduced it in a fresh offline environment.
Independent accuracy and learning effectiveness are not established by those checks.
